▚ 中国传统木作 · CHINESE JOINERY

榫卯 — the joints that hold furniture together without a single nail

A rotatable, explode-able atlas of traditional Chinese mortise-and-tenon (榫卯 sǔnmǎo) joints. Each one is modelled from solid blocks — including tapered dovetails and round members — and rendered from scratch with no 3-D library. Pull the 拆解 / explode slider to separate the pieces and watch the hidden tenon come out of its mortise. Pick any joint from the gallery below.

怎么画出来的 · The renderer. 每个零件由一组凸多面体块组成:轴对齐的方料是长方体, 燕尾的斜面用六面体(两个梯形面)表示,圆材用棱柱近似。卯眼(孔)由"长方体减长方体" 生成——一次布尔差最多分解成六个小长方体,足够表达这里所有方形卯眼。渲染时每个面根据顶点算出朝外法线, 背面剔除,再按深度从远到近绘制(画家算法),明暗是法线与固定光源的一次点积。整套渲染器没有用任何三维库。

In English. Every piece is a set of convex blocks: axis-aligned members are boxes, dovetail tapers are hexahedra built from two trapezoid quads, and round members are prism approximations. A mortise is carved by subtracting one box from another — a single box-minus-box splits into at most six boxes, enough for every square mortise here. Each face gets an outward normal from its vertices, back-faces are culled, and the survivors are painted far-to-near with one dot-product of shading. No 3-D library.

为什么值得做 · Why it's here. 榫卯是"用极少的规则生成严整结构"的绝佳例子—— 一个凸块加一次布尔差,就能穷举出面接合、丁字交叉、三维复合三大类几十种做法。这与本站其它项目里 "从少量生成元推出整个群/层级"的思路是同一种乐趣。

老实话 · Honest note. 复杂的曲线榫(如霸王枨、鲤鱼榫)和三维斜接 (粽角榫)在这里做了几何简化,重在示意其咬合关系而非精确的工程公差;实际制作请以专业图纸为准。
