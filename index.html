FLAGSHIP · ML + Engineering

This is a 0. The model says it's a 6.

A pinch of noise too faint to see flips a confident MNIST classifier completely — that's an adversarial example. Build one live in your browser (FGSM/PGD, L1/L2/L∞), then watch an adversarially-trained model refuse to be fooled. Robustness curve + live decision-region slice.

MATH · POLYNOMIAL OPTIMIZATION

Wrapping a shape in a polynomial

Give it a cloud of points and a polynomial learns their shape: its sub-level set wraps the data and hugs tighter as the degree grows — even non-convex shapes with holes. Pick a shape or draw your own.

MATH · MY SPECIALTY

Polynomial optimization in 3D

A 2-variable polynomial over a curved feasible region, shown as an interactive 3-D surface. Raise the moment/SDP relaxation order and watch the certified lower bound climb until it kisses the true minimum — Lasserre's hierarchy, made tangible. Bounds from a real SDP solver.

ML · DATA AUGMENTATION · SVM

Buddy mixup: pull every point toward its nearest enemy

Every point gets a deterministic buddy — the nearest point of another class, found with a k-d tree — and moves up to half-way toward it. Drag the mixup strength from 0 to 1 on two-moons or two-circles data and watch a live-trained SVM decision boundary reshape itself.

Proves: k-d tree nearest-neighbour search · mixup · SVM (SMO) written from scratch · live decision boundaries
